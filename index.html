Private macOS utility

A calmer way to publish video stories.

Maktaryn YouTube Uploader helps its owner prepare finished videos and schedule their release on the MAKTARYN and UNSPOK YouTube channels. It runs locally on a Mac as part of Story Studio.

Explore the workflow ↘How data is handled ↗

Made for personal use. There is no public account or hosted upload service.

01 / WORKFLOW

From a story folder to a scheduled release.

01

Gather

Read a finished video, its thumbnail, and the title, description, hashtags, and tags from local files.

02

Connect

Use Google OAuth to let the owner connect the intended YouTube channel. The refresh token stays in macOS Keychain.

03

Schedule

Upload the video privately, apply the thumbnail and metadata, set a publication time, and confirm the schedule through YouTube's API.

02 / CONTROL

Local files. Owner-controlled publishing.

The uploader sends selected content directly from the Mac to Google and YouTube. Its website is only an information page; it does not accept uploads or Google sign-ins.
